# WarpView

Read-only web map of a Warp beam. It opens `.warp` output — `beam.json`, `STATUS.md`, `schedule.json` — and shows the tracker, timeline, gates, and ticket table from the Forge-QA build map, retargeted at Warp.

No save. Mark started and mark merged stay in the browser. S and M tickets are the auto-merge set. L and XL wait for approval.

Open `ui/build-map.html`.
